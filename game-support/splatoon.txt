====== Splatoon Game Support Status ======
{{:pretendo:game-support:2_mostly_supported.png?nolink&120|Mostly supported}}
{{:pretendo:game-support:public.png?nolink&120|Public}}

==== Supported Features ====

    * Regular Battles
    * Ranked Battles
    * Squad Battles
    * Private Battles
    * Shops
    * Plaza Posts

==== Unsupported Features ====

    * Splatfests
    * Posting to Juxtaposition on SSSL
    * SplatNet website

==== Current Issues ====

No issues.

==== Modifications ====

    * Rotation lengths are reduced to 2 hours on Pretendo Network, from 4 on Nintendo Network
    * No Rank-based matchmaking in Ranked/Squad Battles
    * [[https://forum.pretendo.network/t/splatoon-banned-stages-weekend-discussion/4714?u=gamersinternatio|Occasional events are hosted enabling users to play banned stage+mode combinations in Ranked Battles]]
