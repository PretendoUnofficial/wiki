====== 3DS Error-Code 015-5015 ======

To access Juxtaposition on your console, link a PNID to your console (assuming Nimbus is set to connect to Pretendo).

You can link a PNID by opening System Settings > Nintendo Network ID settings whilst connected to Pretendo in Nimbus. Then, you can link an existing PNID or make a new PNID.

Once you've entered your credentials or you've finished the setup process, you should now be signed in and you should be able to access Juxtaposition.
