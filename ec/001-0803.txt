====== 3DS Error-Code 001-0803 ======

It looks like you are using a custom DNS whilst trying to access a Nintendo Network service (probably for Wiimmfi).

Disable your custom DNS and the service should work normally. You can do this in ''System Settings > Internet Settings > Connection Settings > Select a connection > Change Settings > DNS'' and set auto-obtain DNS to ''Yes''.
