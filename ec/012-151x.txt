====== 3DS Error-Code 012-1510 and 012-1511 ======

Both of these error codes usually indicate an issue with the certificates for Juxtaposition.

For the former, it generally indicates that the certificate is corrupted. For the latter, it indicates that no certificate was found.

In both cases, these issues can be resolved usually by reinstalling Nimbus on your console.
