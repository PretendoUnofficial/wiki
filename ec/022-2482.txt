====== 3DS Error-Code 022-2482 ======

This game isn't supported on Pretendo Network at this time. Please check out the [[:game-support-status|Game Support Status page]] for a list of currently supported games.

If the game you're attempting to play is supposed to be supported and it's a tester-only game, make sure [[https://forum.pretendo.network/t/-/4955|you're a donator at the Super Mario tier or above]] and make sure your account is configured to connect to beta servers.

/* Just to remind people, whitelisting is NOT required as 022 codes are related to your NNID/PNID rather than ACT I believe which is 002 */

If it's not a tester-only game, make a support post on the forum with the title ID for the game that you're trying to play online for your region.
