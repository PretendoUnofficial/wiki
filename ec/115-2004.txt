====== Wii U Error-Code 115-2004 ======

Juxtaposition isn't available on SSSL.

To use Juxtaposition, install the Aroma custom firmware on your Wii U using the instructions at https://wiiu.hacks.guide. Then, once you've finished that, get Inkay installed with the instructions on https://pretendo.network/docs/install/wiiu and change your DNS settings to auto-obtain.

If you have any problems with modding your Wii U, you can get support on [[https://discord.gg/nintendohomebrew|Nintendo Homebrew]] or [[https://gbatemp.net|GBAtemp]].
